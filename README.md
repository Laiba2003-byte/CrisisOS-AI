#CrisisOS AI

CrisisOS AI is an AI-powered emergency response command center designed for disaster management agencies, rescue organizations, and emergency operation centers. During disasters such as floods, earthquakes, fires, or road accidents, emergency teams receive hundreds of reports from different sources, making it difficult to prioritize incidents and allocate resources efficiently.

CrisisOS AI centralizes all incoming incident reports into a single dashboard, automatically analyzes them using AI to identify the type of emergency, severity, location, and urgency, and then recommends the most suitable rescue teams, ambulances, or emergency resources based on availability, distance, and priority. It also provides a live operational view with maps, resource tracking, incident status, and AI-generated explanations for every decision.

Unlike a chatbot that simply answers questions, CrisisOS AI acts as an intelligent decision-support platform, helping emergency coordinators reduce response times, optimize resource allocation, and make faster, data-driven decisions during critical situations. For the hackathon, the system will use realistic simulated emergency data to demonstrate how AI can improve disaster response workflows.
